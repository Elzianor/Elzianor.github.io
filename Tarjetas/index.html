Tarjetas — Spanish flashcards

Last updated 2 October 2026

Spanish flashcards from your own word list: add the words you actually meet, drill them until they stick, and share them with a class or a friend if you want to. No sign-up, no password and no advertising, and the drill works with no connection at all.

Getting it

  • iPhone and iPad: Tarjetas on the App Store
  • In a browser, on a computer or a tablet, with nothing to install: tarjetas.elzianor.com
  • Android: in a closed test on Google Play before it is published there. Write to the address below to join it.

Help and privacy

Support answers most questions, among them what differs in a browser, and the privacy policy says exactly what the app keeps and what it sends.
