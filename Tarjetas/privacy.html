Privacy Policy for Tarjetas

Last updated 20 August 2026

Tarjetas is an app for learning Spanish with cards. It collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no account to sign in to. This policy says so in the detail Google Play asks for.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no tracking, and no third-party service of any kind. The developer receives no data about you or your device, and has no means of doing so.

Why that is not merely a promise

The Android app requests no internet permission. Android grants network access only to apps that declare it, so the operating system itself would refuse any attempt by Tarjetas to send data anywhere. There is nothing in the app that asks.

It also requests no other permission: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or identifiers. The only permission in its manifest is one Android's own support libraries add for their internal use, which grants no access to anything of yours.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you put into Tarjetas stays on your device, in storage private to the app that other apps cannot read:

  • the words and translations you add
  • your progress through them
  • your settings

None of it leaves the device by any action of the app.

Android backup

Tarjetas allows Android's own backup, which is on by default for most devices. That means Android may include the app's data in the backup it makes to your Google account, so that your words survive a new phone. This is done by Android, under Google's privacy policy and your own backup settings, not by this app or its developer, and the developer has no access to it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. You can also clear it from Android's app settings without uninstalling. Since none of it was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing elsewhere to delete and no request to make.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this, the policy will be updated here before that version is published, and the date at the top will change with it.
